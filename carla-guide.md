# Lantern enquiry desk: Carla's guide

The desk reads the website form and info@ every 5 minutes, day and night. It sends each new person the right first reply and adds them to one shared list with an owner and a next step. You don't need to type anything into the list. Your job is the cases it can't finish alone.

## Your page

Open the link saved on your phone's home screen. It opens on **Needs you**, your to-do list for the day: repairs to book, "whose unit is it?" questions, no-heat cases, people who wrote again with new information, and anything from Rob's photos to check. When you've dealt with one, tap **Done, clear flag** and it leaves the list.

The other tabs:
- **New** shows everyone nobody has moved forward yet.
- **Still open** shows everything not finished, including quotes waiting for a yes or no.
- **All** is the full history, with search.

Tap a phone number to call, or an email address to write. If you've just been told about a new enquiry and want to see it now, tap **Check now** at the top.

## Hand an enquiry to someone else (about 1 minute)

1. Find the person on your page. Search by name if it's quicker.
2. Tap the **owner** box (it shows Carla) and choose Sam or Rob.
3. You'll see "Passed to Sam, email sent". Sam gets an email with every detail, and the next step on the card changes to say it's his.

Remember: businesses always go to Rob, never Sam.

## Change what a reply says (about 5 minutes)

1. Open the **Lantern enquiries** Sheet (Google Sheets app or a computer) and go to the **Replies** tab.
2. Each row is one reply, and column B says when it's used. Change the **Subject** (column C) or the **Body** (column D) like any text.
3. Don't change column A (Key). The desk uses it to find the reply.
4. Words in curly brackets get filled in for you, so keep them as they are: {first_name} becomes the person's first name, and {next_workday} becomes something like "on Monday".

The next reply that goes out uses your new wording. Nothing else to save or press.

## Moving an enquiry along

Use the **status** box on each card: New, Waiting on customer, Visit booked, Quote sent, Won, Lost, Not for us. A quote stays in **Still open** until someone marks it Won or Lost, so none get forgotten. **Add note** and **Change next step** are there for anything else.

## What Rob does

When Rob takes a call, he emails the inbox with "Call" in the subject and the name, number and what they want. For his notepad or a sign-up sheet, he emails a photo. The desk adds each person and emails him "Got it: 2 added". Those rows always land in **Needs you**, marked "Check with Rob", because handwriting can be misread.

## If something looks wrong

If the desk stops checking for 30 minutes, you'll get an email called "Lantern enquiry desk needs a look". Tell Rob, who'll pass it to whoever set the desk up. The top of your page also says when it last checked. Until it's fixed, info@ still works the old way.
